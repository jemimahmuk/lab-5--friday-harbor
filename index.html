About this place

Friday Harbor is the main town on San Juan Island in Washington State. Most visitors arrive on a Washington State ferry from Anacortes. The harbor is full of sailboats, and the waters around the island are known for orcas. This soundscape imagines a day at the harbor: waves, gulls, the ferry, rigging in the wind, a bell buoy and an orca surfacing.
